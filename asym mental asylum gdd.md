a game inspired by forsaken in a mental asylum theme

the players have a Sanity bar that does the following: 
1) when the sanity bar gets too low a client sided character of the killer spawns in which chases the players.
2)   when the sanity bar gets too low the players vision is shortened 
3) when the sanity bar gets too low false sounds of a player being killed is played (which is also reflected on UI) and this is reverted when sanity bar goes up
4) sanity bar drains when in close contact with the killer, can also be drained with an effect


killers: 
## **The Orderly**

- **“Restrain”** – Short lunge that briefly slows a survivor if it hits
- **“Focus”** – Reveals the nearest low-sanity survivor for a few seconds
- **“Presence”** – Standing near survivors drains sanity faster
- **Passive:** Survivors below a sanity threshold leave clearer tracks
